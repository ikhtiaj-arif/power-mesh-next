How it works

One loop from outage window to delivered kilowatts.

  1. Step 1

    Event published

    An operator schedules an outage window with total capacity.

  2. Step 2

    Request or offer

    Consumers request kW. Approved providers publish priced offers.

  3. Step 3

    Allocation

    The operator matches requests to offers within price and capacity.

  4. Step 4

    Pay and deliver

    Pay with bKash, then confirm delivery when power is supplied.

Who it is for

Join as a consumer or provider. Operator and admin desks are invited.

Consumers
Request backup kilowatts for an outage window, reserve an offer, pay, and confirm delivery.
Providers
Apply with your company and capacity. Once approved, publish offers and fulfill allocated reservations.

Operators schedule events and run allocation. Admins manage users and audit history—both are provisioned, not self-serve.

What you get

Capacity when the grid is down
Reserve backup kilowatts for a known outage window—not standing contracts.
Priced per kWh
See offer prices before you reserve. Settle in BDT with bKash.
Audited matching
Operators allocate against rules. Admins keep a full audit trail.
